Reading rules:
- Before reading any date, work out the document's date convention from its country, currency, address format and language; do not assume month-first.
- Look for an unambiguous date on the same document (a day value above 12) and apply its day/month order to every other date on that document.
- When converting to year-month-day output, map the day and month components explicitly and check that they did not swap.
- Sanity-check the invoice date against related dates such as due date, payment terms, delivery date or service period; a swapped day/month often breaks that consistency.
- Read each date component digit by digit, and never fill one component by copying the value of another.
- The vendor is the issuer or seller, found in the letterhead, logo area, "from" or "remit to" block; never take the bill-to, ship-to, customer or any other company named on the page.
- Copy the vendor's identifying text exactly as printed in the issuer block, without substituting a different company name found elsewhere.
- On retail receipts, the business name is usually the capitalized line at the very top, often near a registration or tax number; extract it rather than leaving the field blank.
- Never return an empty field when the document contains the value. Search the header, the footer, and the area around the transaction details before giving up, even on faint or low-quality scans.
- For the total, take the final amount payable after tax and rounding (labels such as total, grand total, nett or amount due), not the subtotal, cash tendered or change.
